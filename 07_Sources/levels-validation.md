# The only real validation is people paying

**Source type:** article

**Author/person:** Pieter Levels

**URL:** [https://levels.io/idea-validation/](https://levels.io/idea-validation/)

**Date published:** 2014

**Date reviewed:** 2026-05-23

## Summary

Levels argues that payment behavior is stronger validation than compliments or stated intent.

## Key Takeaways

- Push toward payment or a strong proxy for payment as early as feasible.
- For enterprise workflows, pilots can be a staged proxy when immediate self-serve payment is unrealistic.

## Claims Worth Importing

- Treat willingness to pay as the strongest validation signal.
