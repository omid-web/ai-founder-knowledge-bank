# Pick your compass metric

**Source type:** article

**Author/person:** Tobias Lutke

**URL:** [https://tobi.lutke.com/blogs/news/11280913-pick-your-compass-metric](https://tobi.lutke.com/blogs/news/11280913-pick-your-compass-metric)

**Date published:** 2013

**Date reviewed:** 2026-05-23

## Summary

Lutke argues for choosing one compass metric and building a tight weekly operating cadence around learning and next actions.

## Key Takeaways

- A founder dashboard should have a small number of decisive metrics.
- Weekly cadence matters: what happened, what was learned, what changes next week.
- A one-person AI company should measure leverage and market proof, not activity.

## Claims Worth Importing

- Use a weekly compass metric to keep AI experimentation tied to business outcomes.
