## Why This Person Matters

Chip is useful for grounding agent and LLM enthusiasm in system design, data, evaluation, and deployment fundamentals.

## Best Lessons For The Builder

- AI engineering is an application discipline: data, context, evaluation, latency, cost, and reliability shape outcomes.
- A builder should understand enough of the stack to make good product and architecture decisions.
- Fast iteration still needs rigorous measurement and failure analysis.

## Concrete Practices To Adopt

- Maintain an AI engineering learning backlog: evals, retrieval, data quality, latency, cost, deployment, monitoring.
- Before adding an AI capability, write the system constraints and acceptance criteria.
- Track cost, latency, and failure modes alongside output quality.

## What Not To Over-Copy

- Do not confuse model access with system competence.
- Do not skip fundamentals because tools make demos easy.
