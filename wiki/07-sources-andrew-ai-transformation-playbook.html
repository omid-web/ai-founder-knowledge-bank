Introducing the AI Transformation Playbook

**Source type:** article

**Author/person:** Andrew Ng

**URL:** https://medium.com/@andrewng/introducing-the-ai-transformation-playbook-58ccad4393e9

**Date published:** 2018

**Date reviewed:** 2026-05-23

Summary

Ng describes a playbook for organizational AI transformation built around pilot projects, training, strategy, and communication.

Key Takeaways

  • Start with feasible pilots that build momentum.
  • AI transformation requires capability-building, not just tool access.
  • A solo founder can compress the enterprise playbook into personal operating rituals.

Claims Worth Importing

  • Use pilot projects and training loops to become AI-native deliberately.
